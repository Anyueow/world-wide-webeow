# Story frame

T-0.6. What the site is actually saying, and why each game symbol is in the
game. Derived entirely from `BIOGRAPHY.md`, which is derived from
`fact-ledger.md`. Nothing new enters here.

---

## The thesis, one sentence

**She builds rooms.** Everything she has made is a group of people assembled
around a problem, and the résumé is what that looks like written down.

## Why the game is the right form for this

This was Ananya's idea and it turns out to be structurally correct, which is
worth saying because it is easy to treat a game as decoration.

A résumé makes you read a person in the order they choose. A room does not work
that way. You walk in, you notice things, you ask about the one that catches
you, and the person becomes real through the objects around them rather than
through a chronology.

Clicking pixel objects to find out which ones are her IS being shown around a
room. The form matches the thesis. That is why it should not be a novelty
layer on top of a normal portfolio, and why the reveal copy is the most
important writing on the site rather than a tooltip.

## The three beats

1. **Come in.** Name, and an invitation to play. No credentials yet. Someone
   should be clicking within four seconds of landing.
2. **Find out who she is, by picking.** The board. Each true symbol unlocks one
   real thing. The decoys are the jokes that make the true ones land harder.
3. **See what that adds up to.** The scorecard, then the honest scroll: the
   work, the proof, how to reach her. This is where a recruiter who does not
   want to play gets everything anyway.

## The one line to lead with

Draft, from her own words rather than mine:

> I love bringing people together, having deep conversations, and solving
> problems. Everything below is a room I built around one.

Second option, tighter, if she wants less explanation:

> Most of what I have made is a room full of people and a problem.

Both are DRAFT. Her actual sentence was warmer and less composed than either,
and there is an argument for using it exactly as she typed it.

## Why each symbol is in the game

The reveal copy in `src/content/game-items.ts` is the short version. This is
why the symbol earns a slot at all.

| Symbol | What it is doing in the story |
| --- | --- |

| DJ headphones | Proof she does things badly first and keeps going. |

### The decoys, and why they are not filler

Ananya wrote all three herself, and they are better than mine were because each
one is true-adjacent rather than random. A decoy that is merely absurd teaches
nothing. Hers each carry a real fact in the correction:

| Decoy | The fact hiding in the joke |
| --- | --- |
| Ribbons | She is not artsy. At all. Worth knowing about someone who brands every project before building it: the branding is strategy, not craft. |
| Glasses | The glasses are blue light, the vision is 20/20. A small correction to an assumption people make. |
| A sea turtle | She has never scuba dived and wants to. The only symbol on the board that is about the future rather than the past. |
| Golf, a TV, a 5am alarm | Mine, not hers. Straight jokes about the shape of her week. Cut them if the board gets crowded, hers are stronger. |

## What the site must never sound like

In 2024 she drafted a bio starting "Meet Ananya Shah, a tech enthusiast who's
always excited about creating solutions that make an impact," and then wrote
underneath it, to herself: "reduce third person, and less passive voice."

That first sentence is the failure mode. Every voice rule in `CLAUDE.md` exists
because she caught herself writing it.
